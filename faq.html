Frequently asked questions

A note on accuracy: some answers below describe nurse employment pathways and fees that may be updated over time. Intermed will confirm the current requirements for your situation.

Who are we? +

Intermed Language Institute, Inc. is a leading German language school for nurses in the country, with eight branches nationwide. Our teachers are native German speakers and are DAF certified to teach nurses; some of them are licensed B2 examiners.

Do we have employers? +

Yes. We cater to both hospitals and nursing homes all over Germany, so you and your nurse friends have a range of employers to choose from.

Do we have agencies? +

No need to look for an agency. We have Athena Nursing Care as our primary partner agency, which is in charge of employers in Germany. Our employers are already waiting for you.

We also have POEA-licensed partner agencies like Bison to help process your documents and your deployment to Germany.

The employers will interview and assess you, especially in the B1 and B2 courses, to come up with employment and salary offers.

What is the duration of the full German language course? +

The A1 Beginner course online class is 120 hours. At 2 hours daily and 5 days weekly, that is 12 weeks.

The A2 Elementary German online class is also 120 hours. At 2 hours daily and 5 days weekly, that is 12 weeks.

The B1 Intermediate German physical class is 140 hours. At 4 hours daily and 5 days weekly, that is 7 weeks.

The B2 “Pflege” Professional Level for Nurses physical class is 220 hours. At 4 hours daily and 5 days weekly, that is 11 weeks.

The total duration of the whole German language training is 8 to 10 months.

What exams do you need to pass? +

Though there are no major exams from A1 to B1, you are still required to have satisfactory performance in quizzes, exercises, and homework to pass each level. The only major exam we prepare you for is the B2 exam. Right after you finish the B2 course, we provide a free intensive review and a free B2 exam on the first take.

How is Intermed different from other schools? +

Besides teaching the full German language course, Intermed Language Institute prepares RN students for the cultural differences to expect when moving abroad, the medical terms and medical role plays in a German setting with German standards. Intermed also offers an intensive review for the German nursing exam and a communication refresher course, which is a big help for those who do not practise speaking while waiting for deployment.

Most of our teachers are licensed B2 examiners as well. They can prepare nurses for the B2 exams because they know the formats and usual topics.

Are you also a recruiting agency? +

No. Intermed Language Institute is a language training centre that prepares you for work in Germany. We have partner agencies for recruitment: Athena Nursing Care Philippines (consultancy service), Athena Nursing Care Germany (will process employment in Germany), and Bison Management Corp (will process POEA/OEC).

How long do we have to study the language? +

Normally 8 months, with classes 4 hours daily, Monday to Friday, covering the A1, A2, B1, and B2 levels. Course length and class format can vary by branch, so please confirm the current schedule with your preferred branch.

What are the required documents needed? +
  • Birth certificate (1A form from the Civil Registrar Office)
  • Passport (at least 2 years before expiry date)
  • Diploma
  • Certification of passing
  • Board certificate
  • Transcript of record (with total number of hours per subject for all minor and major subjects)
  • RLE
  • Certificate of employment (with total number of work hours per department)
  • Seminars and trainings
  • 2x2 picture with white background

Please note that we will be needing the original copies.

Aside from the registration fee, what other fees should we prepare for? +

Travel insurance of ₱5,000, payable once your work visa has been approved.

Is the visa fee free, or do we pay it ourselves? +

The nurse pays the €75 visa fee upon the visa interview at the German embassy. The visa fee is refundable.

Are there any hidden fees? +

No, there are none.

Is the registration fee refundable? +

No, it is not.

What if we fail the qualifying exam to proceed to the next language level? +

You will have to retake the level. The first retake will be free. Any retake beyond that will be charged 50% of the tuition fee.

What if we fail the B2 exam? +

The first take of the B2 exam is free, but to retake the exam you would have to pay the actual rate for the examination fee.

You may retake the exam right away if there is a scheduled B2 exam. If there is none, you must join the B2 class of the next takers. There is no limit to taking the exam.

What is the process after schooling? +

After passing the B2 exam, we can start processing your Recognition in Germany. The B2 language certificate is needed for Recognition, which is why we wait for nurses to pass the B2 exam before applying. Once we have the Recognition and other required documents, we can schedule your embassy appointment and wait for your visa approval and flight to Germany.

Upon deployment, is our flight to Germany free? +

The international flight cost will be covered, but for the domestic flight, the nurse will have to shoulder it independently.

Is there an age requirement or age limit? +

We accept all nurses below the age of 40. Applicants above 40 are required to pay the cash bond and are subject to resume review.

Can nurses choose their employers and locations? +

Nurses may not choose their employer, but may opt to be placed in a desired region depending on availability and vacancy.

Can we bring our family? +

Yes. Nurses have to coordinate with their respective employers once they are already in Germany. The employers will help with the documents needed for the visas of family members, such as financial capacity and permanent residency permits.

We have cases where family visas were approved in less than a year of processing.

How soon can nurses apply for permanent residency? +

After 5 years. The nurse must have the financial capability of renting an apartment under their own name and responsibility.

Are we allowed to go home or have a long vacation within 1 year upon deployment? +

Yes. You have 30 vacation days free to travel.

How long until we have interviews for employment? +

As early as your first few weeks in A1 and as late as after your B2 exam, you will already have had your interview and have an employer.

How long is the waiting period for deployment after schooling? +

It varies between 6 months and 1 year, depending on the employer and whether all your paperwork has been completed in time.

After the visa interview, how long is the waiting period for deployment? +

It can be as soon as 2 to 4 months.

Can we transfer to another employer before completing the 2-year contract? +

As per POEA guidelines, nurses have at least a 2-year contract with their employer. This means they have to stay with their employer for 2 years, but we can take into consideration the situation, problems, and other factors that make them decide to transfer.

We have agents assigned in Germany whom you may contact to address these concerns.

Before recognition, what is our work position? +

All incoming Philippine nurses start as nursing assistants until they pass the official German Nursing Board Exam.

The nurses’ German Nursing Board Exam fees will be shouldered by the employer. The exam can only be taken a maximum of two times. If the nurse fails the exam, they have 18 months allowance left to stay and work in Germany as a nurse assistant.

How much is the starting salary? +

€1,900 to €2,400 before recognition, and €2,400 and above after recognition.

Salaries stated on contracts are monthly gross pay, based on actual offers from employers.

Are there any other deductions from the salary? +

There are no deductions from Athena Nursing Care, but there are tax deductions, as well as accommodation and health insurance costs. Tax deductions range from 30 to 35% of gross salary.

What are the usual arrangements with employers on accommodation in Germany? +
  • Some employers offer 1 month of free accommodation or more.
  • Some employers deduct accommodation from salary at a rate affordable for the nurses.
  • Some employers provide a monthly accommodation allowance.
